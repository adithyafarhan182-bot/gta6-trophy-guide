    Roadmap to the platinum

    Eight stages, built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Stages 1–3 are the story and the open world; stages 4–7 are the grind, done in any order; stage 8 is the cleanup that ends in the platinum.

      Missable trophies

      Story trophies cannot be missed — chapters and missions complete in order, and the finale waits until you are ready. Only one trophy in the whole list needs planning around, and it is the meditation set.

      Sources & method

      The judge cannot play the game, so this guide shows where every fact comes from. Names, descriptions and grades are the official list; the steps are our own advice from those descriptions, not yet checked in play. Where the official description names no place or method, the guide says so instead of inventing one.
